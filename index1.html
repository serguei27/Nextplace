When companies call us

Opening abroad from a distance is where projects slip.

Three signs your opening needs someone on the ground, on your side.

You don't know the local market

Which area, which landlord, which lease terms are normal? Brokers push what they have — not what you need.

Sign: you've seen ten spaces and still can't compare them.

Too many parties, nobody in charge

Landlord, architect, contractors, permits — each with their own plan, and none accountable for your opening date.

Sign: every meeting ends with "we're waiting for someone else".

The date and budget keep moving

Works drift, extras appear, the budget grows — and you find out late, from a photo on WhatsApp, not a report.

Sign: you can't say today what the final cost will be.

Common questions

Answers, straight.

Who do you work for?

For you — the company moving in. We act as your project representative. Any referral commission from a partner is disclosed to you in writing.

Are broker, architect and contractor fees included?

No. Our fee covers our project management. Partner fees are quoted separately, compared and approved by you before anyone is appointed.

Who inspects the works?

Where works need inspecting, a named local professional attends. We agree with you who attends, how often and what they verify — before you start.

Do we need to travel?

Rarely. We coordinate viewings, meetings and follow-up on the ground and report to your HQ every week. You come for the key decisions — or the opening.

Can you work with our own architect or brand standards?

Yes. We coordinate your appointed architect or designer and apply your brand standards to every space.

Where do you work, and in which languages?

Primarily in English, French and Italian, across Europe, Africa and the Gulf. Other countries are possible with local partners — we confirm before we agree the scope.

What determines the fee?

The country, the size and type of space, the stages covered, how many sites, and how many site visits are needed. You get the fee in writing before starting.
