Common questions

Answers, straight.

Who do you work for?

For you — the company moving in. We act as your project representative. Any referral commission from a partner is disclosed to you in writing.

Are broker, architect and contractor fees included?

No. Our fee covers our project management. Partner fees are quoted separately, compared and approved by you before anyone is appointed.

How do you keep control when we are not there?

Every stage runs on the 5Cs Framework™: the scope is contained, there is one plan, every decision has an owner and a date, vendor dates are confirmed rather than assumed, and you receive a weekly report. Where works need inspecting, a named local professional attends — we agree who, how often and what they verify before you start.

Do we need to travel?

Rarely. We coordinate viewings, meetings and follow-up on the ground and report to your HQ every week. You come for the key decisions — or the opening.

Can you help with housing for staff who relocate?

Yes. We brief partner brokers on staff housing, organise viewings, review the leases and coordinate move-in — alongside the office or shop, so your team arrives to a home and a workplace that are both ready.

Can you work with our own architect or brand standards?

Yes. We coordinate your appointed architect or designer and apply your brand standards to every space.

What determines the fee?

The country, the size and type of space, the stages covered, how many sites, and how many site visits are needed. You get the fee in writing before starting.
